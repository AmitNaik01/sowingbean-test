About Us

Our language institute specializes purely in teaching German - not only as a language, but more importantly as a culture and lifestyle. We work in collaboration with the finest tutors and trainers, both native and non-native speakers, who are passionate about all aspects of teaching this language. Our trainers are cheerful, passionate and committed to ignite the same passion in our students. Which means that your courses will incorporate more than just speaking German! In our institute you will have the chance to get to know, enjoy and pass on the culture, traditions and lifestyle of Europeans! We offer the best German courses with the finest guide. Our German faculty was an expat in Germany for 9 years. He has a Master's degree (M.S.) in Technical Logistics from the University of Duisburg Essen (Germany) and has worked for German companies (REWE, FSB Backwaren GmbH). Students will benefit from his extensive academic and professional experience in Germany.
